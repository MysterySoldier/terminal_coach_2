Privacy Policy — Over Your Shoulder

Effective date: November 18, 2026
Developer: MysteryLabs.Dev
Contact: admin@mysterylabs.dev
App website: https://mysterylabs.dev/coach_echo

Over Your Shoulder is a Linux terminal trainer. You learn Linux commands by doing them, with a coach (Coach Echo) guiding you. This policy explains what the app collects, what it does with it, and what it never does.

What we collect

Guest mode: nothing leaves your device. If you use the app without signing in, your progress is stored only on your device.

Google sign-in (optional): if you choose to sign in with Google, we receive your name and email address from Google, and we store your learning progress (which lessons and commands you have completed) in our database so your progress follows you across your devices.

What we do not collect: we do not collect your location, your contacts, your photos, or voice recordings. The app plays pre-recorded audio lessons; it does not record your voice.

How we use it

To sign you in, to sync your progress across your devices, and to run the app. Nothing else.

What we never do

No ads. We do not sell your data. We do not share your data with anyone, except the services that run the app: Google (for sign-in) and Cloudflare (for hosting and the progress database). Those services only process your data to run the app.

How long we keep it

Your progress data is kept while your account exists. To delete your account and your progress, email admin@mysterylabs.dev and we will remove it.

Children

This app is intended for adults (18 and over). We do not knowingly collect data from children under 13.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date at the top. Continued use of the app after a change means you accept the updated policy.

Contact

Questions about this policy? Email admin@mysterylabs.dev.
